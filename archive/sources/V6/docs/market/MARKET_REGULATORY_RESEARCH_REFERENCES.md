## V6 universal-platform research additions

Architecture/market references to verify during M0:
- Odoo Apps + Studio: broad modular business suite and governed customization reference.
- Microsoft Dataverse / Power Apps: standard + custom tables, business rules, model-driven apps and solution lifecycle reference.
- ServiceNow App Engine: enterprise workflow application platform and low-code governance reference.
- SAP clean-core / side-by-side extensibility: upgrade-safe extension architecture reference.
- ONDC / Beckn: discovery/order/fulfillment/payment and buyer/seller/logistics network separation reference.
- GS1 Global Traceability / EPCIS 2.0 / Digital Link: product/location/traceability interoperability reference.
- ISA-95 / IEC 62264: enterprise ↔ manufacturing operations boundary reference.
- OPC UA: industrial information exchange reference.
- MQTT 5.0: lightweight IoT messaging reference.
- ISO 20022: financial messaging integration reference.
- Peppol: e-invoicing/e-procurement interoperability reference.
- ISO 3166 / ISO 4217 / Unicode CLDR: global country/currency/locale reference.
- ISCO / ESCO: occupation/skill mapping reference.
- OpenAPI / AsyncAPI / MCP: HTTP/event/agent-tool interoperability reference.
- WCAG 2.2: accessibility target.

These references guide interoperability; they do not automatically confer compliance/certification.

  https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/manifest-reference/permissions/
