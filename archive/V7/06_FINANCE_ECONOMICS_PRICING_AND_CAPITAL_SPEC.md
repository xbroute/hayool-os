# Hayool OS V7 — Finance, Economics, Pricing & Capital Specification

## 1. Purpose

A technically impressive platform can still fail financially.

Hayool must treat product economics as an authoritative system, not a spreadsheet afterthought.

## 2. Separate financial truth layers

### Customer/tenant operational finance
Ledger, AR/AP, payments, costs, project economics.

### Hayool product unit economics
Revenue/COGS/contribution by product/revenue stream.

### Hayool company accounting
Actual company financial statements.

Do not conflate them.

## 3. Revenue-stream P&L

Track separately:
- SaaS/license
- Cloud hosting premium
- Dedicated
- On-Prem support
- AI Credits
- implementation/onboarding
- modules/packs
- premium SLA
- marketplace share
- extension hosting/runtime
- partner/certification
- network/success fees
- managed contractor/team/outcome
- transaction orchestration.

For each:
billings, recognized revenue, cash collected, direct COGS, variable delivery cost, contribution margin, allocated overhead, working capital.

## 4. Operating financial model

Before Commercial GA create conservative/base/upside scenarios for:

- tenants
- users
- ARR/ARPA
- logo retention
- GRR/NRR
- sales cycle
- CAC
- CAC payback
- onboarding cost
- support cost
- infra/storage/egress
- AI usage/cost
- payment fees
- implementation burden
- marketplace GMV/take rate
- managed workforce volume
- worker payout
- DSO
- reserves/bad debt
- staffing
- R&D
- G&A
- runway.

All assumptions have owner, date, evidence and sensitivity.

## 5. AI economics

Fully Loaded AI Cost:
provider
+ gateway
+ retrieval/embedding
+ tools
+ retries
+ orchestration compute
+ storage/observability where material.

Store:
provider/model/version, raw usage, cost, tenant, feature, workflow, credits charged, result, latency, outcome reference.

## 6. AI Credits

Credits abstract commercial value from raw provider tokens.

Requirements:
- versioned conversion schedule
- plan-included amount
- overage
- budget cap
- soft alert
- BYOK policy
- fallback model
- provider cost-change simulation
- minimum AI margin.

No “unlimited AI” without bounded fair-use/economic policy.

## 7. Pricing policy

Management controls:
- objective
- gross margin floor
- contribution margin floor
- target fully loaded margin
- AI margin floor
- max discount
- allowed experiment range
- renewal/notice policy.

AI cannot redefine the objective.

## 8. Pricing Lab

Every experiment:
- hypothesis
- population
- control
- variant
- randomization
- success metric
- guardrail metrics
- sample/min-duration logic
- legal/trust review
- result
- decision.

Evaluate downstream retention/LTV/margin, not only conversion.

## 9. Managed workforce working capital

Model:
client payment terms
→ collection probability
→ payout timing
→ statutory/partner cost
→ dispute/replacement reserve
→ bad debt
→ financing need.

Do not promise payout behavior the balance sheet cannot support.

## 10. Marketplace economics

Track:
- GMV
- developer gross
- Hayool share
- payment fees
- refunds
- fraud/dispute
- support
- certification/review
- runtime cost
- contribution margin
- developer retention.

The optimal take rate is an experiment, not an intuition.

## 11. Finance product invariants

- debit = credit
- posted entries not silently mutated
- idempotent payment webhooks
- reproducible rule version
- explicit currency/unit
- deterministic tax/payroll/commission
- project profitability reconciles to source facts.

## 12. Project economics

For the initial ICP calculate:
- planned cost
- actual labor cost
- accepted/billable effort
- contractor/vendor cost
- AI/tool cost
- infra/pass-through
- rework
- PM/QA/review
- discounts
- collected revenue
- contribution margin
- estimated fully loaded margin.

Do not label allocated estimates as accounting net profit.

## 13. Financial readiness gate

Commercial GA cannot pass without:
- actual product COGS measurement
- support/onboarding baseline
- AI cost measurement
- pricing sensitivity
- cash/working-capital model
- reconciliation tests
- owner-approved margin policies.
