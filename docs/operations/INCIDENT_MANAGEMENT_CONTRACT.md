# Incident Management — operational contract

Owner: Operations Incident module. REQ-WRK-019, ADR-028, TEST-REQ-WRK-019 and INC suite. Incident records an actual service/delivery disruption. Risk describes a possible future loss; issue describes a work defect/blocker; support ticket is a customer request; security incident follows a restricted specialist workflow. Links among them are typed, not state equivalence. The incident may have project, affected service/customer, asset and ticket links; the security case has its own ACL and may expose only a minimal reference to ordinary operations.

State: `detected -> triaged -> assigned -> mitigating -> resolved -> reviewed -> closed`, with reopened and false-positive branches. Severity matrix uses impact, urgency, customer/asset criticality and current evidence, not AI confidence. Required immutable timeline events: occurrence/detection/report times, owner/incident commander and transfer, affected parties/services/assets, status, communications audiences/channels/content approval, mitigation action, recovery validation, resolution, root-cause hypothesis/evidence, postmortem, corrective/preventive follow-ups with owners/dates, and final reviewer. Sensitive security facts remain restricted. Timeline distinguishes estimated and verified timestamps and records edits by compensating correction. No irreversible deletion of active evidence, retention/rights review still applies.

Customers get only authorized scoped impact and communication. Notification timing and legal reporting are jurisdiction/policy outputs, never a generic clock claimed to satisfy law. SLA clock has an explicit incident reference and own calculation; resolving incident does not silently close tickets or post compensation. Asset expiry or provider outage can trigger, but not grant vault credentials. P0/P1 incident may trigger engineering/release kill switches and on-call routing under approved policy; AI can draft status/postmortem but cannot close, notify externally or declare legal report complete without approval.

TEST-REQ-WRK-019: V1.0 simulate outage affecting two clients, one service, one asset, linked risk/issue and a restricted security case. Verify severity assignment/reclassification, one timeline per scoped tenant, owner handoff, authorized customer messages, mitigation/resolution with proof, root-cause and postmortem approval, follow-ups to closure, and distinct risk/issue states. At V1.1 add linked support ticket and independently calculated SLA clock without cascading closure. Inject late event, provider failure, duplicate alert, wrong-tenant observer, inaccessible security detail, restore/reopen. Pass only with no unauthorized disclosure, no dropped timeline event, and independently reproducible incident duration and evidence chain. Incident outcome evidence is not a worker-performance score.
