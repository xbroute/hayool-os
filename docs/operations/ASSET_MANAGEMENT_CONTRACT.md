# Asset Management — independent operational contract

Owner: Assets module, REQ-TRU-011, ADR-029, TEST-REQ-TRU-011 and AST suite. Separate from Secrets Vault and industrial EAM. Typed asset classes: domains, hosting plans, servers/cloud resources, repositories, DNS zones/records, SSL/TLS certificates, licenses/subscriptions, API/integration accounts, gateways, devices/equipment where applicable. Model parent/child/dependency graph with cycle detection and provider identity; no inferred ownership from a DNS or Git URL.

Fields: tenant/legal entity, owner and responsible person/team, provider, client/project/service relation, financial cost/currency/period/cost center, purchase, renewal, expiry, warranty and proof, lifecycle/criticality, location/region, contract/document refs, versioned audit. Permitted states: `proposed -> acquired -> active -> renewal-due/maintenance -> retired/transferred`, with lost/expired/suspended exception and explicit owner acknowledgment. Digital asset may have a *secret reference ID*, never a raw token/password/certificate private key in the register, export, search index or general AI context. Certificate metadata/public chain may be indexed; private material belongs only in Vault.

Renewal reminders derive from exact expiry/notice windows and current owner; idempotent send, escalation and documented opt-out. A late renewal or owner departure creates an actionable risk/incident/ticket linkage, not an unapproved purchase. Lifecycle retains historical assignment/contract for financial audit under retention policy. Access is tenant/entity/client/project scoped; external customer sees only approved inventory projection. EAM physical maintenance and industrial-control commands remain separate UNI contract.

TEST-REQ-TRU-011: register one of each class, associate related client/project/legal entity and provider, set costs/warranties, renew/expire/transfer, retire and restore. Verify reminder exactly once, overdue escalation, cost-center link, audit history, scoped A/B client view, and that secret values cannot appear in asset JSON/search/export/log. Evidence binds asset snapshots and clock fixture; tested separately from Vault grant/rotation.

Asset records may carry cost basis and a qualified fixed-asset reference, but depreciation/amortization and disposal journals belong solely to the later Finance REQ-FIN-030 and its independent `../finance/FIXED_ASSET_ACCOUNTING_CONTRACT.md`. Operational warranty/renewal does not prove an accounting schedule.
