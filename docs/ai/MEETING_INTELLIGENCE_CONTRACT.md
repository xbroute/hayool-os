# Meeting Intelligence — end-to-end contract

Owner: Knowledge/Meeting; REQ-WRK-010/024/025/029, ADR-030, TEST-REQ-WRK-010/024/025/029 and MEET suite. Manual/AI-off path exists from V1.0; optional transcription/semantic processing may activate earlier than V1.5 only after all first-use AI/provider, privacy, cost, country and owner gates. Recording consent and lawful basis are context-specific policy results, not a hard-coded universal assumption.

Flow: meeting scheduled/imported -> policy check and participant consent state/version -> recording, separately imported audio/video, or manual notes -> provider-neutral transcription where enabled -> segment/speaker/time/source provenance where supported -> versioned summary -> decision, task, requirement, risk and follow-up **drafts** -> configured authority review/approval -> linked domain commands -> audit/feedback/correction. Absence of speaker diarization or timestamps is labeled; never invent a speaker, quotation or precise source offset. Language/direction metadata support Persian and English, mixed-language text, RTL/LTR transcripts and accessible correction UI. Live streaming is optional, not required for a basic imported-file flow.

Media and derivative policy: tenant/entity/meeting ACL, attendee notice/consent per pack, capture pause/withdrawal, device and processor permissions, upload safety, encryption, configured retention, deletion/hold, export, data residency/transfer, revocation and derived artifact revalidation. An unknown/denied capture policy stops recording; manual permitted notes remain available. Sources are immutable references with content hash/time range and speaker confidence, but personal media payload remains deletable or held under reviewed policy. Transcript correction yields a version and invalidates dependent draft confidence; approval on stale source hash is denied. A meeting never grants wider access to a linked project or CRM record.

TranscriptionPort and SemanticPort select eligible local/private or external adapter by capability, language, region, data class, consent, terms and budget. Unknown provider availability => manual. External AI is optional and cannot authorize commands, fabricate citations, provide legal consent or silently create authoritative task/requirement/risk. Human or explicitly configured accountable approval policy binds payload/source/policy version; disallowed high-impact automation cannot be enabled through tenant configuration. Two reviewers may be required for sensitive meeting decisions. Summary may be manually authored and linked to source even with AI off.

Transcript/summary quality requires language-specific error and groundedness evaluation on consented synthetic/approved data before an AI claim. There is no promise of perfect transcription or automatic legal compliance. Meeting evidence is access controlled separately from resulting approved work.
