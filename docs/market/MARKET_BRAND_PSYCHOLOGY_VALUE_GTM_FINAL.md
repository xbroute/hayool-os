## Category and differentiated mechanism

Long-term architectural category hypothesis: **Extensible Business Operating Platform** with governed, optional AI. The earlier phrase “AI-native” describes an intended capability, never a requirement that every task use an LLM or that AI is mandatory. Initial sales category hypothesis: an operating system for project-based digital/professional service companies. Do not lead with “all industries,” “all countries,” “40 modules,” or a replacement claim for a named competitor.

The strategic loop is **Intent → Work/Capability → Resource → Execution → Transaction/Economics → Outcome → Learning**. Core integrity connects these steps. Hayool Studio and the Developer Platform extend the loop without Core forks; the Global Policy Engine controls which steps/corridors/providers may execute in a jurisdiction. The initial proof is a client request becoming approved scope, feasible team, completed/accepted work, reconciled budget/ledger/project contribution and an evidenced improvement decision. The loop is a design differentiation target until measured against alternatives, not a market-leading claim.

## Initial ICP and long-term position

Start with software houses, digital/web/mobile agencies, design/branding and marketing/SEO firms, AI/software consultancies and other project-based professional services. Qualifying pains: fragmented client-to-delivery handoff, capacity uncertainty, mixed employee/contractor work, budget/margin blind spots and upgrade anxiety. V6's illustrative 5–200 worker segment and V7's proposed 3–5 design partners are discovery hypotheses, not validated thresholds or signed customers. Actual geography, buyer authority, sales cycle, contract size and segments require owner-backed research. Long-term breadth includes industry and country packs, commerce/physical operations and ecosystem partners; individual market readiness is always capability- and jurisdiction-scoped.

## Persona psychology and value propositions

| Persona | Job and anxiety hypothesis | Value proposition and tangible proof to test |
|---|---|---|
| Founder/economic buyer | Deliver profitably; fears migration failure, hidden cost and a tool that adds administration. | Client need to project contribution in one audited journey; compare estimate, committed budget, ledger actual and cash without fabricated ROI. |
| Operations/PMO champion | Plans resources and handles exceptions; fears opaque AI and lost handoffs. | Capacity, risks, incidents, meeting decisions and acceptance linked to source; can override proposals with authority trail. |
| Finance/controller | Needs exact books, control and explainable variance; fears the platform treats forecast as actual. | Exact journal, approved budget/cost center and independent reconciliation; export and period close. |
| Ordinary user | Wants to know the next task with minimal training; fears crowded ERP screens and surveillance. | Role workspace, clear next action, accessible forms/notifications, progressive disclosure and correction/undo path. Do not expose SDK or rule engine as the default experience. |
| Enterprise buyer/security/IT | Fears lock-in, data loss, vendor dependency, broken upgrade, residency and secret leakage. | Scoped API/export, restore/upgrade test, permission manifest, Cloud/Dedicated/On-Prem evidence, AI-off/private routing and country capability matrix. These are claims only after proof. |
| Developer/partner | Fears unstable APIs, arbitrary review, copying of apps, no discoverability/economics. | Stable public contracts, synthetic sandbox, conformance test, consent version, upgrade survival, transparent review/IP/monetization and appeal. |
| Worker/talent | Fears underbidding, opaque ranking, invasive monitoring, auto-rejection and uncertain payment. | Clear scope/rate/consent, eligibility before search, fair exposure, explanation/appeal, human review and no universal public human score. |
| Client of tenant | Fears scope drift, unclear progress and unexpected invoice. | Scoped portal approvals, evidence-backed changes, support/SLA and reconciled commercial terms. |

These anxieties are hypotheses for interviews and behavior research; they must not be presented as measured prevalence or exploited through dark patterns. Test accessibility and language with ordinary users, not only administrators.

## Brand personality, architecture and messaging

Masterbrand working name **Hayool OS**. Studio, Developer Platform, Marketplace, Network, Cloud and Insights are surface descriptors; independent subbrands require actual market pull and owner decision. Character: capable, calm, precise, humane, ambitious with evidence, culturally adaptable and respectful of control. The product should sound helpful and accountable, not omniscient, bureaucratic, surveillance-oriented or obsessed with AI.

Messaging ladder:

1. Plain outcome: “From client need to accountable delivery and economics.” Persian wording is localized and tested, not mechanically translated.
2. Explain mechanism: one traceable loop linking work, resources, execution, transactions and outcomes.
3. Show control: approvals, budget/ledger reconciliation, human choice, AI-off, export and recovery.
4. Show extension: create a workflow in Studio or install a scoped app; no fork of Core.
5. Show global adaptation: approved packs and providers per capability/corridor, never “worldwide compliant.”

For a developer: “Build an app against public contracts, with explicit scopes and upgrade proof.” For talent: “Relevant eligible work with clear scope and reviewable decisions.” Feature names and module counts support navigation and procurement, not the brand promise. Final slogans, translations, trademarked marks and published claims require owner approval.

## Demo narrative and adoption

The internal demo follows one real or consented synthetic company: client request → discovery/meeting → approved requirements/scope → budget/discount-controlled estimate and contract → capacity/team → work/time/risk/incident → client acceptance → invoice/partial collection → cost/profit center and journal-backed actual → project contribution and outcome learning. Show a denied permission, AI-off manual path, failed provider reconciliation and one extension without touching Core. Do not pretend a simulated transaction is a live country capability.

Product-led growth hypothesis: self-service safe trial/sandbox, a guided first project and first team/quote, import preview and export confidence; activation is first completed meaningful operating loop, not account creation. Privacy-conscious analytics measure task completion, time to first value, recurring workflow use, error/abandonment, support effort and opt-out; metrics cannot force tracking beyond approved purpose. Free/low-cost tiers and AI credits are unapproved commercial design options, not public offers.

Founder-led/design-partner motion: recruit a small, diverse set of ICP partners; agree data, feedback cadence, support and permissible case-study use; observe real golden journey, onboarding friction, budget/profit accuracy, trust objections and willingness to renew. Do not label discounted beta as product-market fit. Land-and-expand begins with project/economics/CRM use, then appropriate portal, capacity, talent, AI, Studio, country/industry packs as measured needs and verified policy allow. A failed regulated branch does not block unrelated SaaS growth but cannot be marketed as delivered.

## Pricing psychology, economics and trust

Separate SaaS, deployment/support, modules/packs, AI credits/BYOK, network/managed, marketplace and implementation terms. Show billable unit, usage caps, forecast, overage/approval, renewal/grandfathering, refund and export costs before commitment. Prevent surprise model/provider cost changes from silently changing a contract. Do not hide implementation/support burden or use confusing module bundles to force dependence. Discounts honor business floor and independent legal/fair compensation floor; vulnerable worker pressure or underbidding race is not a growth tactic. Pricing experiments pre-register cohorts, stop criteria and harm measures; owner approves live price/terms. Unit economics distinguish billings, cash, recognized revenue, GMV and contribution, with sourced assumptions. No fabricated savings/payback promise.

Lock-in anxiety: prove documented export, stable APIs, reversible configuration, upgrade/restore and exit path. AI anxiety: default human approval where consequential, explicit provider/data/cost controls, local/private path where eligible and complete AI-off journey. Privacy/security anxiety: show tenant isolation, scoped access, deletion/retention choices, secrets boundary and independently tested recovery; a design document is not a security certification. Developer ecosystem trust: transparent app review, permission re-consent, compatibility window, IP/clone conflict policy, suspension appeal and payouts only under approved terms.

## Localization and competitive frame

Localize meaning, evidence, accessibility, date/money labels, direction and trust language per audience and approved Country Pack. Persian RTL and English LTR are early experience targets; a translated page does not prove lawful local operation. Other markets can use domestic, regional, allowlist or global eligible sourcing modes only through pack facts/approval. The provider/country capability page shows `architected`, `simulated`, `verified`, `enabled` distinctly.

Competitive research is by buyer workflow and alternative category (project tools, ERP/finance, HCM, talent marketplaces, automation/AI, app builders). Test the closed-loop hypothesis against actual buyer alternatives: integration friction, migration effort, total cost, time-to-value, trust and extension upgrade survival. V6 named-vendor comparisons are historical hypotheses requiring dated official/vendor evidence and customer interview before external use. Do not claim unique features, competitor deficiencies, superiority or pricing without a current documented comparison method and legal/marketing review. Retained V6 insight: Studio, agents, marketplace or ERP modules alone are not differentiation; the connected proof is the candidate differentiator.

## Claim, trademark and release gates

Claims registry fields: exact wording/translations, persona/channel, product/version, jurisdiction/capability, hypothesis or measured status, methodology/cohort/sample, current source and limitations, owner approver, effective/expiry, evidence IDs and withdrawal route. Prohibited without scoped proof: “compliant,” “certified,” “global,” “real-time,” “autonomous,” “escrow,” “EOR,” “AI decides,” “guaranteed margin/ROI,” “for all industries,” or unqualified security/accessibility. A demo script and pricing page are publications subject to the same registry. An expired evidence link unpublishes or qualifies the claim.

The Hayool name, word mark, logo, product surface names, domains and handles require a target-market clearance plan before public brand investment/launch: relevant classes/services and languages, confusing similarity, local/national/regional registers, domain/app-store/handle checks, qualified counsel opinion, ownership and re-check date. [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) is a research starting point; [USPTO comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) illustrates why a single exact-name web query is insufficient. No actual clearance is claimed here. Owner selects launch markets and authorizes name/filing strategy; negative clearance finding blocks affected public use, not internal technical documentation.

Market-readiness evidence: real internal use V1.0; independently operated design partner V1.1; actual problem/alternative/willingness-to-pay interviews and measurable onboarding/repeat use; finance and support cost baseline; observed retention and upgrade/export confidence; accessibility and security product evidence; signed legal/provider pack only for claims in those corridors; owner-approved price/contract/capital and trademark gate. V1.6 claim is scoped to initial ICP and enabled capabilities; V1.10 partner distribution needs independent app survival/economics proof. A numeric design aspiration above 9.5 is a target, never an Evidence Readiness score for absent implementation, customer or legal observations.
