# Hayool OS — Global Product Experience & Persona Workspaces
Version: 7.2 overlay (V6 detail adopted)
Status: Adopted normative detail; subject to V7.2 resolutions and release mapping

---

## 1. Core experience principle

A platform this broad fails if users experience it as "hundreds of modules."

Hayool OS must expose a simple role- and intent-oriented interface over a broad capability platform.

---

## 2. Two universal entry modes

### Intent-first
A user can type/say what they want:

- "I need meat delivered by 7."
- "Hire a sales rep."
- "Move stock to branch 2."
- "Create a website for my company."
- "Why is profit down?"
- "Repair the compressor."

Hayool turns the request into a proposed plan.

### Workspace-first
Expert users navigate structured modules/tables/processes.

Both lead to the same underlying objects and policies.

---

## 7. Public / consumer UX

For public users:
- no ERP vocabulary
- natural-language intent
- simple options
- price/ETA
- tracking
- support
- account/order history.

---

## 8. Enterprise UX

Enterprise requires:
- dense tables
- bulk actions
- keyboard
- saved views
- filters
- export
- approval queues
- audit drilldown
- multi-company context.

Beauty must not reduce operational efficiency.

---

## 9. Mobile / field

Mobile optimized for:
- scanning
- photo
- signature
- GPS where consented
- quick status
- offline queue
- task completion
- delivery proof.

---

## 10. Kiosk / POS

Dedicated simplified surfaces for:
- store checkout
- warehouse station
- reception
- factory terminal
- visitor/customer self-service

through policy-controlled kiosk sessions.

---

## 11. Accessibility / localization

- Persian RTL first-class
- LTR first-class
- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- keyboard
- screen-reader semantics
- touch targets
- low vision
- color-independent status
- localized number/date/unit/currency.

---

## 12. Low bandwidth

Global reach requires:
- lightweight pages
- image optimization
- incremental loading
- offline/degraded flows where relevant
- retry/resume.

---

## 13. Error psychology

Do not expose infrastructure jargon.

Errors answer:
- what happened?
- what was saved?
- what can user do?
- will retry happen?
- whom to contact?

---

## 14. AI trust UX

AI output visually distinguishes:
- fact
- calculation
- prediction
- recommendation
- draft.

Show:
- sources/evidence
- confidence where meaningful
- why
- edit/reject
- action preview.

---

## 15. Customization

Admin may:
- branding
- theme tokens
- workspace modules
- navigation
- dashboards
- fields/forms
- terminology.

Customization must preserve accessibility/security.

---

## 16. Onboarding

AI-guided discovery:
- business type
- country
- size
- branches
- workflows
- products/services
- team
- modules.

System recommends a starting Solution Pack.

Do not present every possible module during initial setup.


## 17. Builder / developer experience

Non-programmer:
- visual Studio
- AI-assisted draft
- preview/test
- publish/rollback.

Developer:
- Developer Portal
- test tenant
- app console
- logs
- permissions
- installations
- versions
- billing/analytics.
