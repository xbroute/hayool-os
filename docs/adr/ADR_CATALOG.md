## Reserved owner decisions (not engineering ADR acceptance)

`OWNER_DECISIONS.md` records ODR-001–014: employing/payment/custody structure, launch jurisdictions, provider/contracts and data transfer, live compensation/prices/capital/SLA, commercial and brand/trademark claims, labor/AI/meeting review, real budget approval basis, use of personal data across tenants, hosting regions, marketplace economic terms and actual authorized signatories. Technical defaults above may be changed by evidence-backed ADR without owner interruption except where they would alter a reserved decision. Each applied decision needs approver identity/date/scope and effective policy version.

## ADR review mechanics

An ADR implementation task cites test proof, rollback plan, deployment mode and operational owner. A replacement records supersedes/superseded-by, why, migration cost and affected REQs. Technical selection alone never proves license availability or a provider's contract. All accepted decisions are pre-code design, and exact dependency versions are deliberately frozen only at implementation inventory.
