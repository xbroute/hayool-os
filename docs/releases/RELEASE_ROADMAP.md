Gate owner is an accountable role to staff, not an invented person. Entry needs previous dependent gates and unexpired security/tenant/finance proof; known P0/P1 or missing mandatory evidence blocks. ## Dependency and scope rules

V1.4 is a regulated branch. If blocked, V1.5 intelligence for permitted SaaS operations and V1.6 SaaS-only GA may progress after their own gates, with managed mode disabled and no V1.4 pass asserted. V1.7 can be tested privately before V1.6 but cannot inherit a failed commercial claim. Each expanded jurisdiction, engagement/payment mode, provider/data route and marketplace listing needs fresh scoped evidence; global architecture is not global launch permission. Every gate includes negative/cross-tenant/revocation/failure/recovery tests applicable to its REQs. A named capability in an early release is part of the demonstrated journey rather than a claim that all advanced variants are complete.

Any external AI processor used for meeting transcription, candidate matching or any other earlier journey must pass REQ-AI-003–007 as applicable and ODR-006 before its **first** production call; otherwise that branch remains manual/deterministic/AI-off. V1.5 expands and proves intelligence at scale, not a retroactive waiver. V1.8 device proof includes REQ-UNI-014; V1.5 Digital Twin proof includes REQ-AI-015. V1.0/1.1 establish the small Studio data/package foundation; V1.7 demonstrates full no-code builder and independent upgrade proof.

## After V1.10

V2 candidate outcome gates, to be sequenced only with measured demand and qualified boundaries: deeper WMS/TMS and intercompany accounting; asset/IoT/edge operational replay; agricultural trace and recall; advanced industry packs; partner-governed banking/regulated integrations; hosted extension runtime only after sandbox threat model and independent proof; cross-tenant aggregate learning only with consent/privacy proof; new country corridors and accessibility mappings. Each retains the REQ-UNI-013/AI-014/GLB contracts; specialist core banking, clinical EHR and safety-critical industrial control require separate systems/qualified partner rather than invented general availability. No V2 date or revenue forecast is approved.

## Gate record template

Gate ID, exact SHA/build/config/pack/provider version, deployment mode and environment; frozen cohort/expected outcome; REQ/test/EVID links; measured results and failure cases; security/privacy/accessibility/legal/finance signoffs as applicable; P0/P1 register and accepted residual risk; owner commercial authorization where needed; rollback and support owner; decision pass/fail/pending with reviewer/date. A design document can pass document completeness review but cannot substitute for an executed product gate.
