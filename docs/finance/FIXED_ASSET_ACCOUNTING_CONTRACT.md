# Fixed-asset accounting and depreciation — V1.9 contract

Status: specified; no statutory method or country readiness asserted. Finance owns REQ-FIN-030 and TEST-REQ-FIN-030. Digital/physical Asset registers own identity, ownership, warranty and lifecycle (REQ-TRU-011 and REQ-UNI-018), never the journal.

An eligible acquisition is reviewed for capitalization by legal entity/book, asset class, currency, acquisition/useful-life/start date, residual value, method and approved applicable Country Pack. The register passes a scoped immutable asset reference; Finance records cost basis, impairment, effective-dated method/schedule revisions, depreciation or amortization period runs, disposal proceeds and ledger posting references. Small operating subscriptions can remain expense under reviewed policy. No country-specific useful life or tax deduction is a Core default.

`TEST-REQ-FIN-030` covers acquisition, two closed periods, change in estimate, impairment, transfer and sale/retirement. Independently recompute expense and accumulated value, require balanced journal and explicit approval; a changed asset name/owner must not rewrite posted history, a revoked asset reference cannot resurrect a disposed schedule, and a late correction uses journal adjustment rather than deleting closed-period truth. Finance/legal reviewers qualify any real book/tax schedule before activation.
