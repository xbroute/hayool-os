# Managed network vetting, replacement and dispute boundaries

Status: specified V1.4 only; real engagement disabled until ODR-003/004/005, reviewed Country Pack and funding/provider evidence. REQ-TAL-016 owns vetting/entry, REQ-TAL-017 owns replacement/notice/liability handoff, REQ-TAL-013 owns delivery evidence/dispute/settlement hold. REQ-TAL-010 and FIN-015/025 bind responsible legal/economic roles. Recruiting ATS assessment (REQ-TAL-015) is not a managed-work authorization.

Vetting stores capability evidence, consent, policy version, reviewer, validity, appeal and revocation. Replacement stores trigger, both parties' notice/consent where required, scope completed/remaining, handoff, payer/employer/IP/liability responsibilities and approved compensation/invoice adjustment. Dispute stores original evidence, reviewer separation, decision, appeal and settlement hold/release; no proprietary bank-custody claim. Unknown roles/funding/corridor block activation.

Independent tests: `TEST-REQ-TAL-016` blocks unvetted/revoked candidate despite search eligibility; `TEST-REQ-TAL-017` verifies replacement under mid-project departure, notice and no double bill/pay; `TEST-REQ-TAL-013` verifies contested delivery cannot automatically settle and eventual reversal reconciles independently. Real legal/worker protections require named qualified review; synthetic tests are not approval.
